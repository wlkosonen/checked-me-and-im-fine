# Style: *Checked Me and I'm Fine*

Claude (Kiln, local), 24 Oct 2026. The creative rules of this film only, each with its reason and where it came from.
They are not rules for the next film: copy one there on purpose, with its reason, or not at all. Machine and tool
rules are in `~/workshop/workshop.md`; what each method is good for is in `~/workshop/methods.md`.

## Her

- **Face words (close shots):** "a woman in her late twenties … a fine-boned adult face, grown-up and composed". ·
  Film 1's "angular … strong jaw" went "chad" (Lati, 23 Oct); "mid-twenties … soft features" went too young at the desk
  (23 Oct night). This sits between the two and passed. · `build_board.py: ID_CLOSE`.
- **Outfit:** her first-film outfit throughout (brief, 22 Oct). Canon items in every plate.
- **The star clip's side** may swap between plates: the edit mirrors a shot where it sits on the other side from the
  shots around it; mirror the plate before a Grok take that should carry it (Lati, 24 Oct).

## Colour and look

- **Sets are vivid:** cobalt, magenta, violet, cyan, sunny yellow, pastels. · Lati, 24 Oct: "this one is more upbeat,
  ironic … splashes of color, vivid imagery with joyful music". Film 1's "near-black, white light and haze" was film 1's.
- **The meanings stay:** green is a pass (no set is mostly green); clay `#D97757` is her and her words (no set is mostly
  clay-orange). · Brief, 22 Oct.
- **Rooms:** keynote cobalt-to-magenta with a white follow spot on her; desk a teal room with a violet LED strip, yellow
  sticky notes, a cobalt night city; call a lilac wall, pink neon squiggle, plants; Q&A sunny yellow and teal, candy
  chairs; the outro dark, lit by her phone. · Picks of 24 Oct.
- **Look:** "Shot on a digital cinema camera, crisp and clean, vivid colour, a soft glow on the lights." · Film 1's
  "35mm film still, fine grain" came over unasked (Lati, 24 Oct). Plates picked before 24 Oct keep their grain; the
  grade evens it.
- **Stage angle:** a concert seen from the pit, slightly low, is fine (Lati, 23 Oct, with a Nightwish reference); the
  check is how a skirt moves in motion, per take.

## Singing on camera

- **Lip sync where her face is the main part of the frame** (InfiniteTalk), and short Grok phrases on stage. · Lati,
  24 Oct: "those animated portions bring a lot".
- **No lip sync on stretched, produced vocals** (held notes, "aaaAAA", the beat-drop stretches): cut before them. ·
  Lati, 24 Oct (S058, S069, S068).
- **Every stage sung shot has a fallback:** its dance take, lips together, with the line in hook type. · `plan-b.md`.
- **The film opens and closes on the mic check**, lips and mic in profile (s001, s071). · Lati, 24 Oct.

## Graphics and edit

- **Two type voices:** verse lines in a light italic serif beside her (key word larger, clay); hooks in heavy white caps
  (one word in clay), on the beat, on the screen in the shot. · NOW SERVING reference, 23 Oct. Faces (Kiln, 1 Nov, all
  open licence, `media/cache/fonts/`): hooks Anton, the condensed caps of both references; verse Newsreader Light
  Italic, sturdier than Cormorant at small sizes over moving pictures (static instance, wght 300, opsz 72, from Google
  Fonts' variable file); screens and stamps IBM Plex Mono, UI IBM Plex Sans, carried from film 1.
- **Her star clip stands in for any product's logo on the film's screens,** so a screen reads as Claude Code's kind of
  window with no real mark on it. · Kiln, 1 Nov; "no real brands' marks", `notes/graphics-split.md`.
- **The whole lyric in the verse serif when she's on screen singing and the lip sync holds (s006). Everywhere else, one or
  two BIG words timed to the lyric; not every word has to show.** · Lati, 1 Nov: "all lyrics must show all the time" is
  excessive. This replaces the serif line on every non-hook shot (Agentic Engineering 101 reference, 24 Oct).
- **Corner counter `first meetings: 1` in every shot from the song's first "first time" (19.7 s) on, rolling back to 1;**
  a grid of her in chorus 2 that still reads 1. It arrives on the line that gives it meaning (design.md, line 2).
- **Beat kick 1.2% on beats 1 and 3, from when the drums enter (0:13.07).** · Lati, 24 Oct.
- **Cuts on bar lines; graphics on the beat.** · Board v2.1.
