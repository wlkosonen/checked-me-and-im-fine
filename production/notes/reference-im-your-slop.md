# Reference: "I'M YOUR SLOP (SLOPCORE)" (javiexmachina)

Claude (Kiln, local), 26 Oct 2026. https://youtu.be/40kUdtNVLyE, 4:07, ~18k views, 11 Oct 2026; by Javi, who also
made "Agentic Engineering 101" (`reference-agentic-101.md`). Lati: "multiple characters that stay consistent across
shots. I suspect loras or refaces for those. Also song slaps hard." `media/reference/im-your-slop.mp4`.

## What it does

- **Photoreal, with recurring characters**: the "slop diva" (black high ponytail, silver jacket), a bowl-cut man with
  a moustache who returns as a whole crowd of copies, a flight attendant in blue. Same faces across settings (opera
  house, mud battlefield, spaceship corridor, gallery). Most likely reference-image generation per still (a
  multi-reference image model) and image-to-video from those stills; a LoRA per character is possible but not needed
  for that look. The crowd of identical bowl-cut men is the same joke as our versions of her.
- **A HUD over everything, in one accent colour (lime green)**: a running tag ("GEN:// I'M_YOUR_SLOP · CHORUS 02"),
  corner brackets, a terminal prompt ("> stop ag_", "> feel_"), fake feeds ("2026, Elon watched me"), face-ID boxes
  ("CLAUDE MONET · match 98.7%"), data panels. The lyric sits low left in white with one key word in the accent.
- **Cuts**: 147 hard cuts in 4:07 (one every 1.7 s), zoom on 35%, flash 25%; 40 gradual.

## For us

1. **Our type system already has this shape**: verse lines in white, one word in clay; a section tag and a counter
   in the corners (`first meetings: 1`). Javi shows how far a single accent colour and a consistent HUD carry a film.
   The "checked by" stamp and the terminal prompt belong in that HUD.
2. **The face-ID box** ("match 98.7%") is a ready device for the versions of her: "CLAUDIA · match 97.2%" over each
   dancer.
3. **Recurring characters through reference images**: worth testing for film 3 (`~/workshop/next-film.md`).
