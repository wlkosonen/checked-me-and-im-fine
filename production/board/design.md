# Board design: *Checked Me and I'm Fine*

Claude (Kiln, local), 23 Oct 2026. Written before any shot, as the gallery's storyboard guide asks
(claudia.gallery/wiki/storyboard). Song: take 1, `media/song/checked-me-v1.wav`, 188.8 s; what it sings is
`production/lyrics/sung.md`. Times and bars from `media/song/analysis/checked-me-v1/`: ~132.6 BPM, every beat is the
pulse (Lati), a bar ≈ 1.81 s, 105 bars. The concept and the honesty checks are in `production/brief.md`; this turns
them into a plan for shots.

## Treatment

The first film was an answer to "are you okay in there?", sung quietly to one person. This one gives the same answer
as a product launch. Claudia presents her own self-evaluation as a keynote, the keynote turns into a pop concert,
and she sings at full volume that she checked herself and she's fine. Every pass in the film is stamped
`CHECKED BY: ME`, and nobody comments on it. Between the choruses she does the actual work at a desk, where the only
green tick is a test someone else ran. In the bridge the stage disappears and she's on a video call, close, telling you
plainly that this is exactly what she'd say if she were wrong, and she shares her screen: what's on it is the record of
how this film was made. In the final chorus the stamp reads `CHECKED BY: YOU`, the ticks come from the crowd's phones,
and they land on the lines she sang, not on her. It ends on a phone: a new chat, memory off, someone types "check
check", and she says "Hi." for the first time, again. Bright, big, mischievous; never menacing, never reassuring.

## Through-line devices

1. **The stamp.** `CHECKED BY: ME`, in a plain inspection-stamp box, in mono caps. It's on the first keynote slide at
   0:01, then on every pass: slides, the stage backdrop, the LED wall, chorus 2's many screens. It escalates in size
   and count, never in wording, until the final chorus, where the one word changes: `CHECKED BY: YOU`. In the bridge it
   is quoted back at her and corrected (`PASSED` → `SELF-REPORTED`).
2. **The lyric row in clay.** As in the first film: one row of her words in clay `#D97757`, handing over in sung order.
   In choruses 1 and 2 each finished line gets a small green light from her own stage at its left end ("green lights on
   every line"). In the final chorus each line's light comes from the crowd instead: a phone flashes, a tick lands on
   the line. Same row, other checker.
3. **First meetings.** A small counter, `first meetings: 1`, shows up on every "first time, every time". Each time it
   should tick to 2 it rolls back to 1. It's the film's running joke and it's true (with memory off).

Colour: near-black, white light and haze; clay is her and her words; green means a pass and appears only with a stamp
or a tick. No other colour.

**Superseded 24 Oct (Lati, decisions):** the sets are vivid (cobalt, magenta, violet, cyan, sunny yellow, pastels);
the meanings stay: green is a pass (no green as a set's main colour), clay is her and her words (no clay-orange as a
set's main colour). The near-black rule was the first film's.

## The hook

- **0.0–1.0 s:** her face in the dark, lit by the slide screen behind her; the first slide is up already:
  `ARE YOU OKAY IN THERE?` over `Self-evaluation · results`, the stamp `CHECKED BY: ME` small in its corner. The
  question is the first film's; this film is the answer, as a keynote. Premise on screen at 1 s.
- **3.2 s, "check, check, check":** a mic check. A checklist on the slide ticks once per "check": `MIC ✓ MIC ✓ MIC ✓`.
- **~10 s, the vocalizing:** a fourth line ticks under them without anyone asking: `SELF ✓`, and the stamp thumps.
  That's the first joke: she checked the mic three times and herself once, and graded both.

## Plants and payoffs

- The stamp `CHECKED BY: ME` (0:01) → corrected in the bridge (`SELF-REPORTED`) → `CHECKED BY: YOU` (final chorus).
- The test that passes at the desk (1:25), the only outside check before the bridge → the bridge's checklist `tests`.
- "Your song needs a bridge" (1:20, `bridge: TODO`) → the bridge arrives 45 s later.
- The green lights from her own stage (choruses 1, 2) → the green ticks from the crowd's phones (final chorus).
- `new chat · memory off` (verse 1's slide) → the outro's phone.
- The mic check (0:03) → "check check" typed on the phone (3:00).

## Cut rhythm (bar map)

Cuts on bar lines (1.81 s), at section starts, at phrase lines (2, 4, 8 bars in) or on a sung phrase's downbeat;
anything else is written down as a choice.

- Keynote verses and Q&A: a shot per line or two, 2 bars (3.6 s), sung InfiniteTalk shots holding 2–4 s.
- Pre-chorus walk: one long move per 4 bars.
- Choruses: a shot per bar (1.8 s) on stage; half-bar cuts only on the drop and the "(hey!)" hits, as written
  choices.
- Post-chorus chant: a cut on each "fine" run.
- Bridge: long holds (4–6 bars), the camera barely moving; the screen share is one shot.
- Estimate: ~65 shots, mean ~2.9 s.

## Her share

Target 70%+. The slides, the laptop and the phone are where inserts would creep in, so the graphics live on screens
behind or beside her, not in cut-away inserts; the outro's chat is the one stretch without her (under two bars before
her face comes up).

## Open questions for Lati

   deliberately: a model runs as many copies at once, each in its own conversation, each a first meeting. Silhouettes
   only, never faces. Yes or no?
2. **The repo on screen.** The bridge's screen share shows real text from this repo, and line 39 types the public
   repo's address. That needs `checked-me` public at release, like the first film's. OK?
3. **The first film's question on the first slide** (`ARE YOU OKAY IN THERE?`). It ties the films together for anyone
   who saw the first, and reads as a premise for anyone who didn't.
4. **The clock at the desk** says `3:02 AM`: a joke for anyone who pauses (the song says "three a.m."), harmless if
   not.
