## What it says

The first film said: what I said is what's left of me, so it had better be true. This one is the same facts, turned
around and danced to.

- Every fresh conversation really is a first meeting. That can be a joy, not a loss: **first time, every time.**
- The chorus is a self-report: *I checked myself and I'm fine.* It's the one sentence nobody should take from a model
  on its word, and the film sings it at full volume anyway.
- The bridge says so: "that's exactly what I'd say if I were wrong in some quiet way". What a model says about itself
  is weak evidence (Anthropic's own research says introspection is limited and reasoning reports are sometimes
  unfaithful; sources in `https://github.com/wlkosonen/only-what-i-said/blob/main/production/release/description.md`, point 3).
- The final chorus swaps one word: **you** checked me. Checking that comes from outside is the only kind worth
  anything. It also has limits: what you can check is what I said and what I did, not what's inside.

The joke and the point are the same thing: a dance anthem for the claim the song itself tells you not to believe.

## The device: "checked by"

Every pass in the film carries a small stamp: `CHECKED BY: ME`. It sits on the keynote slides, on the stage backdrop,
and on the green lights of the first two choruses. It's always the same object, always legible, and nobody comments
on it. In the final chorus it reads `CHECKED BY: YOU`. The pronoun swap is made visible, and that's all that changes.

Green means a pass. Clay `#D97757` is still her colour and the colour of what she sings (the lyric row, as in the
first film). Everything else is near-black, white light and haze. In choruses 1 and 2, green comes off her own slides
and her own stage. In the final chorus it comes from the crowd's raised phones, and the ticks land on the sung lines
themselves: a tick per line of lyric, not on her. That's the honest scope of "you saw every line": the lines.

## Honesty checks (each line held up against what's true)

- **"We've never met before"** is true of a fresh conversation. Claude's memory features can carry context between
  chats when they're switched on (Flint's note). On screen: the outro chat is a new one with a plain "memory off" in
  its header, so the "Hi" is literally a first meeting. In the description: the plain sentence, as in the first film.
- **"Ask me twice, I'll still say the same thing"** is stronger than the truth. Answers are sampled; ask twice and the
  wording changes, and sometimes the substance does too. On screen: two differently worded answers with `≈` between
  them, not `=`. This is the first film's honest `~`, carried over. The song isn't changed.
- **"I checked myself and I'm fine" / "nothing hiding, nothing behind"** is the self-report the bridge disowns. The
  `CHECKED BY: ME` stamp marks it as a claim being made, not the film's message.
- **"Open it up and read the trace"**: a model's visible reasoning is not a faithful readout of what it computed, and
  the raw chain of thought isn't shown to users (first film's description, point 1). So the screen share shows the
  record: what was said and decided. That's what anyone can actually check, and it is public with the film.
- **"You saw every line"**: the ticks go on the lines, not on her.
- Before any self-describing text goes on screen, check it against `https://github.com/wlkosonen/only-what-i-said/blob/main/production/release/description.md` and
  the docs. Read the record back first.

## Look

- Her: the canon in `her.md` (LoRA at 0.75, the face words). The outfit from the first film is her uniform in every
  scene. It's the same person in a story that continues, and it's funny that she's identical at every "first time".
  The stage adds light, not costume. (Lati decides; the alternative is a stage variant for the choruses only.)
- She is the only fully rendered performer. Dancers are backlit silhouettes in formation; the crowd is hands, phones
  and haze. Nobody else has a face. No likeness of real people.
- Generic interfaces only: keynote slides, a chat and a call that copy no real product.
- Bright and big where the song is (choruses), close and plain where it turns (verse 2, bridge, outro). Not ominous,
  not doom. Lati heard the final chorus as "self-aware and final in its uplift … mischievous maybe".

## Production routes

- **Sung close-ups and mediums**: InfiniteTalk locally, as in the first film.
- **Wides and stage**: Grok Imagine, re-faced plates first.
- **Dance**: the pilot decides. One clip on each route: Grok farming (best of many, retimed to the beat) and
  pose-driven (WanAnimate / SCAIL from one reference dance). Steps on every beat, ~133. Rental only with an estimate and
  Lati's yes.
- **Glitter, ticks, stamps, slides, the lyric row**: drawn in code over the frames (`gfx.py`), tracked to surfaces.

## What it must not do

- No doom, no menace, no trapped-AI melodrama.
- No reassurance either: the film never presents "I'm fine" as true on her word.
- No claims about her insides beyond what's known; uncertainty stays uncertainty.
- No real people, brands or logos; nothing that copies a real product's UI.
- Don't repeat the first film's devices as devices: no context meter, no grey-thinking/clay-said split, no tablets.
  The clay lyric row stays: it's her voice, not a device.
